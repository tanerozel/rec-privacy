Rec support

Rec: Voice Recorder Pro — Android, iPhone, iPad, Wear OS and Apple Watch

Questions, problems or ideas: write to tanerozel47@gmail.com. We answer in English and Turkish, usually within two working days.

A recording stopped or is missing

Rec saves audio continuously. If the app was closed during a recording, open Rec again: the recording is restored automatically. Deleted recordings stay in Recently deleted for 30 days.

Transcription

Transcription runs on your device. Download a speech model once in Settings → On-device models (needs a network connection), then open a recording → Transcript → Transcribe. Nothing is uploaded.

Backup

Backup is optional and off by default. Choose a folder (Files or iCloud Drive on iPhone, any folder on Android) or your own WebDAV server in Settings → Cloud backup. Rec never deletes or replaces files there.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
